Direct answer

How does the Linear integration work?

People continue using Linear as their tool. StackOS adds it to a visible workflow, keeps the work in order, and passes only the approved action to the integration when that stage is ready.

  1. 01Your requestStart in your AI
  2. 02StackOS planExpands the steps
  3. 03LinearDoes its bounded part
  4. 04Checked resultKeeps the proof

Common questions

Linear and StackOS.

What can the Linear integration do in StackOS?

The current catalog includes 34 Linear actions across Issue Work. The exact actions used depend on the workflow and approval rules.

Does StackOS replace Linear?

No. Linear remains the tool that performs its part of the job. StackOS organizes the workflow, keeps status and dependencies visible, and calls the tool only when the plan reaches the right approved action.
