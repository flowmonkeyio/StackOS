Direct answer

How does the Taboola integration work?

People continue using Taboola as their tool. StackOS adds it to a visible workflow, keeps the work in order, and passes only the approved action to the integration when that stage is ready.

Common questions

Taboola and StackOS.

What can the Taboola integration do in StackOS?

The current catalog includes 10 Taboola actions across Campaign Management, Creative Operations, Media Measurement. The exact actions used depend on the workflow and approval rules.

Does StackOS replace Taboola?

No. Taboola remains the tool that performs its part of the job. StackOS organizes the workflow, keeps status and dependencies visible, and calls the tool only when the plan reaches the right approved action.
