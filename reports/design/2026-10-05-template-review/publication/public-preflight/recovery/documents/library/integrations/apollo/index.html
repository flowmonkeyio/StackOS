Direct answer

How does the Apollo integration work?

People continue using Apollo as their tool. StackOS adds it to a visible workflow, keeps the work in order, and passes only the approved action to the integration when that stage is ready.

Common questions

Apollo and StackOS.

What can the Apollo integration do in StackOS?

The current catalog includes 5 Apollo actions across Enrichment, Lead Management. The exact actions used depend on the workflow and approval rules.

Does StackOS replace Apollo?

No. Apollo remains the tool that performs its part of the job. StackOS organizes the workflow, keeps status and dependencies visible, and calls the tool only when the plan reaches the right approved action.
