Turn on review reminders for the colleague assigned to your article while it waits for review. Draft Pier emails them at 9 a.m. on weekdays, using your workspace’s time zone, with a link to the draft. Each pending draft gets at most one reminder per weekday. Your colleague decides whether to approve the draft or request changes; a reminder does not approve or publish it. You can enable or disable reminders for each draft.
