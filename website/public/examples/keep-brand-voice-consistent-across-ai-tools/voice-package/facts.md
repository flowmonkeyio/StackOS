# Fictional product facts for this exercise

Record: F-01
Revision: 1.0
Owner: example product lead
Public-use status: invented demonstration material; not a real product claim

- Draft Pier is a fictional app for reviewing article drafts.
- The new review-reminder option sends an email to the assigned reviewer at
  9 a.m. on weekdays, using the workspace's time zone.
- It sends at most one reminder per pending draft per weekday.
- A reminder contains a link to the draft.
- The reviewer can approve the draft or request changes.
- A reminder does not approve or publish a draft.
- The writer can enable or disable reminders for each draft.
- No customer results, performance measurements, pricing or delivery guarantee
  are available. Do not infer them.
