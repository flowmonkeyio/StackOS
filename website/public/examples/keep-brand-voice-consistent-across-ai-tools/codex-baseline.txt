Your article is waiting in harbor. While it’s pending your colleague’s review, you can enable review reminders for that draft. Draft Pier emails the assigned reviewer at 9 a.m. on weekdays, using your workspace’s time zone, with a link to the draft. It sends at most one reminder per pending draft per weekday. Your colleague decides whether to approve it or request changes; a reminder does not approve or publish it. You can disable reminders for that draft whenever you choose.
